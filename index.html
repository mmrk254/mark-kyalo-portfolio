About

Practical software, built for how businesses actually work.

I recently completed a Bachelor of Science in Software Development at KCA University. Since then I've been building full production systems rather than isolated demos — school ERPs that handle curriculum and finance together, POS platforms that run hotel front desks, and payment integrations that move real money through M-Pesa and bank rails.

Most of what I build has to work inside constraints particular to this market: patchy connectivity, mobile-money-first payments, and systems like NEMIS and KNEC that Kenyan institutions are already tied to. Designing around that, rather than against it, is where most of the interesting engineering happens.
